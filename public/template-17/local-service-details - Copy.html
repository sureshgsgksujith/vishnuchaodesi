    About This Service

    Sparkle Pro delivers reliable, professional home and office cleaning across the Los Angeles metro area. Our background-verified team brings their own eco-friendly supplies and equipment to give your space a spotless, healthy finish — whether it is a one-time deep clean, a move-in/move-out service, or a recurring weekly visit.

    With over 12 years of experience and 5,000+ completed jobs, we focus on punctuality, transparent pricing and a 100% satisfaction guarantee. Booking takes less than two minutes and you can reschedule any time, free of charge.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    • Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

      No. Our team arrives with all professional, eco-friendly supplies and equipment. If you prefer we use your products, just let us know when booking.

    • Can I reschedule or cancel my booking?

      Yes, you can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 12 hours before your appointment directly from your dashboard.

    • Are your cleaners background-verified?

      Every professional is background-verified, trained and insured before joining our network.

    • How is pricing calculated?

      Pricing is based on the package and home size. The price you see is the price you pay — no hidden charges.

    • Which areas do you cover?

      We currently serve the Los Angeles metro area, within 25 miles of Downtown LA.
