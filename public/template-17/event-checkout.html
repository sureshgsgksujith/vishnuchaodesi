    About This Event

    Join hundreds of cycling enthusiasts for the Chicago Bike Trails Festival 2026 — a full-day celebration of riding, fitness, and community along the scenic Lakefront Trail. Whether you are a casual rider or a seasoned cyclist, there is a route and an experience crafted just for you.

    The festival features guided scenic rides, live music, food trucks, a gear expo, and family-friendly activities. Expert ride leaders will accompany every group, and support vans will be available throughout each route to keep your day safe and enjoyable.
